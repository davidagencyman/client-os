# Follow-up

Draft a concise, human follow-up from the detailed session record.

## Read

1. the newest detailed session
2. status/actions.md
3. status/decisions.md
4. status/current.md
5. privacy.md
6. context/client.md for communication preferences

## Rules

- Reflect what actually happened.
- Confirm decisions, owners, and next steps.
- Keep unresolved items unresolved.
- Do not add commitments that were not agreed.
- Do not expose internal coaching observations or private third-party detail.
- Match the client's language and relationship.
- Prefer a short message with a clear next move over a full transcript recap.

Run privacy-boundary.md before sending or sharing. Drafting does not authorize
sending.
